3. MODE

Formula (Continuous Series):
Mode = L + [(f₁ - f₀) / (2f₁ - f₀ - f₂)] × h
Where: L = Lower limit of modal class, f₁ = Frequency of modal class,
f₀ = Frequency of preceding class, f₂ = Frequency of succeeding class, h = Class width
Question 5: Calculate the mode:
Marks0-1010-2020-3030-4040-50
Students816282414
Step 1: Identify modal class
Maximum frequency = 28 → Modal class = 20-30
Step 2: Identify values
L = 20, f₁ = 28, f₀ = 16, f₂ = 24, h = 10
Step 3: Apply formula
Mode = 20 + [(28 - 16) / (2×28 - 16 - 24)] × 10
= 20 + [12 / (56 - 40)] × 10
= 20 + [12/16] × 10
= 20 + 7.5 = 27.5
∴ Mode = 27.5 marks

7. PROBABILITY

Basic Probability: P(E) = Favorable outcomes / Total outcomes
Addition Rule: P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A ∩ B)
Multiplication Rule: P(A ∩ B) = P(A) × P(B|A)
Bayes' Theorem: P(A|B) = P(B|A) × P(A) / P(B)
Question 9: A box contains 4 red, 5 white, and 6 blue balls. Three balls are drawn at random. Find the probability that:
(a) All three are red
(b) One of each color
(c) None is blue
Total balls = 4 + 5 + 6 = 15
Total ways to select 3 balls = ¹⁵C₃ = 15!/(3!×12!) = (15×14×13)/(3×2×1) = 455
(a) All three red:
Ways to select 3 red from 4 = ⁴C₃ = 4
P(All red) = 4/455 = 0.0088
(b) One of each color:
Ways = ⁴C₁ × ⁵C₁ × ⁶C₁ = 4 × 5 × 6 = 120
P(One each) = 120/455 = 0.264
(c) None is blue (all from red + white = 9):
Ways = ⁹C₃ = (9×8×7)/(3×2×1) = 84
P(No blue) = 84/455 = 0.185
∴ (a) P(All red) = 0.0088, (b) P(One each) = 0.264, (c) P(No blue) = 0.185

10. REGRESSION ANALYSIS

Regression Line (Y on X): Y = a + bX
Slope: b = Σ(X - X̄)(Y - Ȳ) / Σ(X - X̄)² = [nΣXY - ΣXΣY] / [nΣX² - (ΣX)²]
Intercept: a = Ȳ - bX̄

11. F-TEST (Variance Ratio Test)

F-Statistic: F = S₁² / S₂² (larger variance in numerator)
Degrees of Freedom: df₁ = n₁ - 1, df₂ = n₂ - 1
Decision Rule: If F_calc > F_crit, reject H₀
Question 13: Compare the risk of two portfolios at 5% significance level:
Portfolio APortfolio B
Sample Size (n)2116
Variance (S²)9.84.2
Test whether Portfolio A is significantly riskier than Portfolio B.
Step 1: State Hypotheses
H₀: σ₁² = σ₂² (No significant difference in variances)
H₁: σ₁² > σ₂² (Portfolio A has higher variance - riskier)
Step 2: Calculate F-statistic
F = S₁² / S₂² = 9.8 / 4.2 = 2.33
Step 3: Degrees of freedom
df₁ = n₁ - 1 = 21 - 1 = 20
df₂ = n₂ - 1 = 16 - 1 = 15
Step 4: Critical value (Right-tail test, α = 0.05)
F₀.₀₅(20, 15) ≈ 2.28
Step 5: Decision
F_calc (2.33) > F_crit (2.28)
Reject H₀
∴ F = 2.33 > F_crit = 2.28
Conclusion: Portfolio A is significantly riskier than Portfolio B at 5% significance level.

12. t-TEST

12.1 One-Sample t-Test

Formula: t = (X̄ - μ₀) / (S / √n)
df = n - 1
Question 14: A sample of 15 measurements has mean X̄ = 9.85 cm and S = 0.5 cm. Test at 1% level whether the true mean differs from 10.0 cm.
Step 1: State Hypotheses
H₀: μ = 10.0 (Mean length is 10 cm)
H₁: μ ≠ 10.0 (Mean length differs from 10 cm) - Two-tailed test
Step 2: Given values
X̄ = 9.85, μ₀ = 10.0, S = 0.5, n = 15
√n = √15 = 3.873
Step 3: Calculate t-statistic
S/√n = 0.5/3.873 = 0.129
t = (9.85 - 10.0) / 0.129 = -0.15 / 0.129 = -1.16
Step 4: Critical value
df = 15 - 1 = 14
t₀.₀₀₅,₁₄ = ±2.977 (two-tailed at α = 0.01)
Step 5: Decision
|t_calc| = 1.16 < |t_crit| = 2.977
Fail to reject H₀
∴ t = -1.16, Critical t = ±2.977
Conclusion: We do not have enough evidence to say that the true mean length is different from 10 cm at 1% significance level.
99% Confidence Interval:
CI = X̄ ± t₀.₀₀₅ × (S/√n)
= 9.85 ± 2.977 × 0.129
= 9.85 ± 0.384
= (9.466, 10.234)
Since 10.0 is within this interval, we cannot reject H₀.

12.2 Two-Sample t-Test

Question 15: Compare means of two independent samples:
Sample 1Sample 2
n1012
Mean7568
Variance1625
Test at 5% level if there is a significant difference.
Step 1: State Hypotheses
H₀: μ₁ = μ₂
H₁: μ₁ ≠ μ₂
Step 2: Calculate pooled variance
S²p = [(n₁-1)S₁² + (n₂-1)S₂²] / (n₁ + n₂ - 2)
= [9(16) + 11(25)] / 20
= [144 + 275] / 20 = 419/20 = 20.95
Step 3: Calculate t-statistic
t = (X̄₁ - X̄₂) / √[S²p(1/n₁ + 1/n₂)]
= (75 - 68) / √[20.95(1/10 + 1/12)]
= 7 / √[20.95 × 0.183]
= 7 / √3.83 = 7 / 1.96 = 3.57
Step 4: Critical value
df = 10 + 12 - 2 = 20
t₀.₀₂₅,₂₀ = ±2.086
Step 5: Decision
|t_calc| = 3.57 > |t_crit| = 2.086
Reject H₀
∴ t = 3.57 > t_crit = 2.086
Conclusion: There is a significant difference between the two means at 5% level.

13. Z-TEST

Z-Statistic: Z = (X̄ - μ₀) / (σ / √n)
Used when: Population σ is known OR n ≥ 30
Question 16: A sample of 36 has mean 148.5 cm. Population σ = 5 cm. Test at 1% level if population mean differs from 150 cm. Also find 99% confidence interval.
Step 1: State Hypotheses
H₀: μ = 150
H₁: μ ≠ 150 (Two-tailed)
Step 2: Calculate Z
σ/√n = 5/√36 = 5/6 = 0.833
Z = (148.5 - 150) / 0.833 = -1.5 / 0.833 = -1.80
Step 3: Critical value (two-tailed, α = 0.01)
Z_crit = ±2.576
Step 4: Decision
|Z| = 1.80 < 2.576
Fail to reject H₀
Step 5: P-value
P(Z < -1.80) = 0.0359
P-value = 2 × 0.0359 = 0.072 (7.2%)
Step 6: 99% Confidence Interval
CI = X̄ ± Z₀.₀₀₅ × (σ/√n)
= 148.5 ± 2.576 × 0.833
= 148.5 ± 2.15
= (146.35, 150.65)
∴ Z = -1.80, P-value = 0.072
99% CI = (146.35, 150.65)
Conclusion: Since CI contains 150, we fail to reject H₀. The mean is not significantly different from 150 cm.

FORMULA QUICK REFERENCE

Central Tendency

Mean: X̄ = Σfm/Σf
Median: L + [(N/2 - cf)/f] × h
Mode: L + [(f₁-f₀)/(2f₁-f₀-f₂)] × h

Dispersion

Variance: σ² = Σf(x-x̄)²/N
SD: σ = √Variance
CV: (σ/X̄) × 100

Correlation & Regression

Pearson r: [nΣXY - ΣXΣY] / √[nΣX²-(ΣX)²][nΣY²-(ΣY)²]
Spearman ρ: 1 - 6Σd²/n(n²-1)
Regression: b = [nΣXY - ΣXΣY]/[nΣX² - (ΣX)²], a = Ȳ - bX̄

Hypothesis Testing

Chi-square: χ² = Σ(O-E)²/E
t-test: t = (X̄-μ)/(S/√n)
Z-test: Z = (X̄-μ)/(σ/√n)
F-test: F = S₁²/S₂²
