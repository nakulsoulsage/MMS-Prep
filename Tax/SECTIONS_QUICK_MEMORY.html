⚠️ SECTION 115BBE - UNEXPLAINED INCOME (Remember: "BBE = Black money tax BE 60%")

Tax on Concealed Income:
  • Tax Rate: 60%
  • Surcharge: 25%
  • Cess: 4%
  • Effective Rate: ~78%
Applies to: S.68 (Cash credits), S.69 (Unexplained investments), S.69A, S.69B, S.69C, S.69D

🧠 FINAL MASTER MNEMONIC - Section Number Pattern:
  • Single Digit (2,6): Basics (Definitions, Residence)
  • 10-17: Exemptions & Salary
  • 22-24: House Property
  • 28-44: Business (longest!)
  • 45-55: Capital Gains
  • 56-59: Other Sources
  • 64: Clubbing
  • 72A: Loss carry forward (mergers)
  • 80C-80U: Deductions (Chapter VI-A)
  • 115 series: Special tax rates
