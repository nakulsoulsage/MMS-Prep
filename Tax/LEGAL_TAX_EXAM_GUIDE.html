LEGAL TAX EXAM GUIDE

Income Tax Act, 1961 - Comprehensive Study Guide

1. SECTION 2(24) - DEFINITION OF INCOME

Definition: Section 2(24) defines "income" in an INCLUSIVE manner - not exhaustive, covers items not specifically mentioned.

Income Includes:

  • Profits and gains from business/profession
  • Dividend income from companies
  • Voluntary contributions to trusts
  • Value of perquisites/profits in lieu of salary
  • Capital gains from transfer of capital assets
  • Winnings from lotteries, races, gambling (special rates)
  • Life insurance receipts (except exempt under S.10(10D))
  • Deemed incomes (gifts beyond limits)

5. SECTION 6 - RESIDENTIAL STATUS

Basic Conditions for RESIDENT

A person is RESIDENT if ANY ONE satisfied:

  • Condition 1: Stay in India >= 182 days in previous year, OR
  • Condition 2: Stay >= 60 days in PY AND >= 365 days in preceding 4 years

Types of Residential Status

StatusConditionsTaxability
ROR (Resident & Ordinarily Resident)Resident + (Resident in 2/10 preceding years AND 730+ days in last 7 years)Global income taxable
RNOR (Resident Not Ordinarily Resident)Resident + (NR in 9/10 preceding years OR <= 729 days in last 7 years)Indian income + Business controlled from India
NR (Non-Resident)Does not satisfy basic conditionsOnly Indian income taxable

14. SECTION 72A - AMALGAMATION/DEMERGER

Benefit: Accumulated losses & unabsorbed depreciation can be carried forward by transferee/resulting company

Amalgamation Conditions:

  • Government approval
  • Transfer of ALL assets & liabilities
  • Business continuation for 5 years
  • Transferee holds 75% voting power

Demerger Conditions:

  • Part of business transferred
  • Shareholders get shares in resulting company
  • Both companies continue to exist
  • No sale of shares involved

19. DTAA - DOUBLE TAX AVOIDANCE

Methods of Relief:

  • Exemption Method: Income taxed in only one country
  • Tax Credit Method: Foreign tax credited against Indian tax
  • Deduction Method: Foreign tax deducted as expense

Conditions for DTAA Benefits:

  • Permanent place of abode in treaty country
  • Tax residency certificate
  • Centre of vital interests (family, business, investments)

Permanent Establishment (PE):

Fixed place of business (branch, factory, office) - If PE exists in India, income taxable in India

25. MEMORY TIPS

  • 5 Heads: SHBCO (Salary, House Property, Business, Capital Gains, Other Sources)
  • Residential Status: 182 OR (60+365) = Resident
  • ROR: 2/10 + 730/7
  • RNOR: NR 9/10 OR <=729/7
  • Clubbing: SSWMR (Spouse Salary, Spouse transfer, Wife of Son, Minor, Revocable)
  • Capital Gains: 12-24-36 rule (Listed shares-Property-Others)
  • Presumptive: 44AD=8%/6%, 44ADA=50%, 44AE=7500/1000
